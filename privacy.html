Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Movie Score Card ("the App") handles information when you interact with the App on Reddit.

1. Information We Collect

  • Reddit Account Identifier: When you submit a movie rating, the App records your Reddit User ID or username to enforce a 1-vote-per-user deduplication rule and prevent duplicate ratings.
  • Vote Submissions: The numeric rating score you choose (1 to 10) and the subreddit context where the vote occurred.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect email addresses, real names, passwords, phone numbers, IP addresses, or payment details.
  • We do not track your activity across Reddit or outside the specific score card post.

3. How We Use Information

  • To compute aggregated community audience rating scores and vote totals.
  • To display your recorded vote back to you when you revisit the score card post.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored securely via Reddit Devvit Redis storage and Cloudflare D1 databases over encrypted HTTPS. We never sell, rent, or trade your data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App runs within the Reddit developer platform (Devvit). Your interactions on Reddit are also governed by the Reddit Privacy Policy.

6. Contact & Data Deletion

To request data deletion or ask privacy questions, contact u/nandhugp214 on Reddit.
