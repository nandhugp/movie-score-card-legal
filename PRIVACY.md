# Privacy Policy for Movie Score Card

**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Movie Score Card** ("the App") handles information when you interact with the App on Reddit.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Reddit Account Identifier:** When you submit a movie rating, the App records your anonymized Reddit User ID or username to enforce a 1-vote-per-user deduplication rule and prevent duplicate ratings.
- **Vote Submissions:** The numeric rating score you choose (e.g., 1–10) and the subreddit context where the vote occurred.

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We **do not** collect personal email addresses, phone numbers, real names, passwords, IP addresses, or payment details.
- We **do not** track your browsing behavior outside the specific Reddit post hosting the App.

### 3. How We Use Information
- Compute aggregated community audience rating scores and vote counts for each movie.
- Display your existing vote back to you when you revisit the score card post so you know what score you previously gave.

### 4. Data Storage & Security
- Data is stored securely via Reddit Devvit Key-Value / Redis storage and Cloudflare D1 databases over encrypted HTTPS.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

### 5. Third-Party Services
The App runs within the Reddit developer platform (Devvit). Your interactions on Reddit are also governed by [Reddit's Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy).

### 6. Contact & Data Deletion
If you wish to have your vote records removed from the database, please contact [u/nandhugp214](https://reddit.com/user/nandhugp214) on Reddit.
